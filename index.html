Emmanuel Ruiz is a software engineering manager at Arthrex, a global medical device company whose mission is Helping Surgeons Treat Their Patients Better®. He works on the web platform behind arthrex.com and its family of sites, and on X-Ray, the quality gate that audits them every week and on demand.

This site is where he writes about building software at the frontier: working with AI agents as a team, shipping at a pace that used to be unthinkable, and refusing to let quality be the price of that pace.
